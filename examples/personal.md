# Optional personal instructions

Add any of these to your own CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md after setup.

- Use English and conversational Yorùbá naturally, with emoji where they fit.

## Alárinà usefulness log

- In every session that uses the `alarina` skill, log how useful it was in `~/.qp/alarina/<claude|codex>/<session-slug>.log`. Use `<project>-<checkout>-<YYYY-MM-DD>` as the slug.
- Add one line per Alárinà rule or role you applied: `HH:MM | role/rule | what it made you do | useful? (yes/no/mixed) | note`.
- Log when a rule helped, cost time for no gain, conflicted with another instruction, or was missing.
- When you brief a worker that uses Alárinà, ask it to return its log lines in its report, and write them to the worker's folder (`claude/` or `codex/`) if it cannot write there itself.
